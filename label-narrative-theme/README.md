# Label Narrative WordPress Theme

A conversion-optimized, narrative-driven WordPress theme designed specifically for selling 9×9cm labels (square or circle) to Australian small and micro businesses.

## Overview

This theme uses storytelling principles to make your customer the hero of their business journey, while incorporating proven conversion optimization techniques for maximum sales effectiveness.

## Installation

### Requirements
- WordPress 6.0 or higher
- PHP 7.4 or higher
- WooCommerce 7.0 or higher (for e-commerce functionality)

### Steps

1. **Upload Theme**
   - Download the `label-narrative-theme` folder
   - Go to WordPress Admin → Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload Theme
   - Upload the theme ZIP file
   - Click "Activate"

2. **Install WooCommerce**
   - Go to Plugins → Add New
   - Search for "WooCommerce"
   - Install and activate WooCommerce
   - Complete the WooCommerce setup wizard

3. **Import Demo Content** (Optional)
   - Go to Tools → Import
   - Install WordPress Importer
   - Import the demo content XML file (if provided)

## Configuration Guide

### Initial Setup

#### 1. Site Identity
Navigate to: **Customizer → Site Identity**

- Upload your logo
- Set your site title and tagline
- Choose a site icon (favicon)

#### 2. Hero Section
Navigate to: **Customizer → Hero Section**

Configure the main headline section:
- **Hero Headline**: Main attention-grabbing headline
  - Default: "Your Brand Deserves to Stand Out"
  - Best practice: Keep it under 10 words, focus on customer benefit
- **Hero Subheadline**: Supporting text
  - Default: "Premium 9×9cm labels that tell your story and grow your business"
- **Hero CTA Text**: Button text
  - Default: "Start Your Label Journey"
- **Hero CTA Link**: Where the button links to (e.g., #product or a product URL)
- **Hero Background Image**: Upload a background image for the hero section

#### 3. Story Section
Navigate to: **Customizer → Your Story Section**

Tell your brand story:
- **Enable Story Section**: Toggle on/off
- **Story Headline**: Section title
  - Default: "Every Great Business Has a Story"
- **Story Content**: Your narrative text
  - Tip: Focus on the customer's journey, not your company history
  - Make it relatable and emotional
- **Story Image**: Upload an image that supports your narrative

#### 4. Benefits Section
Navigate to: **Customizer → Benefits Section**

Highlight why customers should choose you:
- **Enable Benefits Section**: Toggle on/off
- **Benefits Headline**: Section title

Configure 4 benefits (each has):
- **Benefit Icon**: Emoji or CSS class (e.g., 🏭, 📦, ⚡, ✨)
- **Benefit Title**: Short title (3-5 words)
- **Benefit Description**: 1-2 sentences explaining the benefit

**Default Benefits:**
1. Proudly Australian Made
2. Perfect for Small Runs
3. Lightning-Fast Turnaround
4. Professional Quality

#### 5. Trust & Social Proof
Navigate to: **Customizer → Trust & Social Proof**

Build credibility:
- **Enable Trust Section**: Toggle on/off
- **Trust Headline**: Section title
- **Trust Badges**: 4 trust indicators (e.g., "500+ Happy Customers", "5-Star Reviews")

Reviews from WooCommerce will automatically display if available.

#### 6. Call to Action Section
Navigate to: **Customizer → Call to Action Section**

Final conversion push:
- **Enable CTA Section**: Toggle on/off
- **CTA Headline**: Compelling final message
- **CTA Subheadline**: Supporting text
- **CTA Button Text**: Button label
- **CTA Button Link**: Destination URL
- **CTA Background Color**: Section background color

#### 7. Product Page Settings
Navigate to: **Customizer → Product Page**

Customize product pages:
- **Product Story Section**: Enable/disable narrative section on product pages
- **Product Story Headline**: Title for the story section
- **Product Story Content**: Narrative content specific to your labels
- **Add to Cart Button Text**: Customize the button text
  - Default: "Get Your Labels Now"
- **Trust Badges**: 3 trust indicators below the add-to-cart button
- **Product Specifications**: Enter specs (one per line)

**Default Specifications:**
```
Size: 9×9cm (90mm × 90mm)
Shapes: Square or Circle
Material: Premium paper stock
Finish: Glossy or Matte
Adhesive: Permanent, strong-stick
Printing: Full-colour, high-resolution
Minimum Order: From 50 labels
Delivery: Australia-wide
```

#### 8. Theme Colors
Navigate to: **Customizer → Theme Colors**

Customize your brand colors:
- **Primary Color**: Main brand color (used for links, accents)
- **Secondary Color**: CTA buttons and conversion elements
- **Text Color**: Main text color

#### 9. Footer Settings
Navigate to: **Customizer → Footer Settings**

Configure footer content:
- **Footer Copyright Text**: Copyright notice
- **Footer Contact Info**: Contact details (email, phone)

#### 10. Menus
Navigate to: **Customizer → Menus**

Create navigation menus:
- **Primary Menu**: Main header navigation
- **Footer Menu**: Footer navigation

## Setting Up Your Products

### Create Your Label Products

1. Go to **Products → Add New**

2. **Product Title**: Keep it clear and descriptive
   - Examples: "9×9cm Square Labels - Premium", "9×9cm Circle Labels - Premium"

3. **Product Description**: Use the narrative approach
   - Start with the customer's problem
   - Explain how your labels solve it
   - Include emotional benefits, not just features

4. **Product Short Description**: Quick summary (appears near the add-to-cart button)

5. **Product Images**:
   - Upload high-quality images showing:
     - The labels themselves
     - Labels on products (in-context shots)
     - Different angles and applications
   - Minimum 3-5 images

6. **Product Data**:
   - Set price
   - Manage stock (enable stock management for urgency triggers)
   - Set shipping details (Australian addresses)

7. **Product Attributes**:
   - Create attributes for:
     - Shape: Square or Circle
     - Quantity: 50, 100, 250, 500, etc.
     - Finish: Glossy or Matte

8. **Variations**:
   - Create product variations based on attributes
   - Set different prices for different quantities

## Customization Tips

### Writing Compelling Copy

#### Hero Section
- Focus on the transformation, not the product
- Bad: "We sell 9×9cm labels"
- Good: "Your Brand Deserves to Stand Out"

#### Benefits
- Always lead with benefit, then feature
- Bad: "We use premium paper stock"
- Good: "Professional Quality - Vibrant colours and durable material that makes your products look premium"

#### Story Section
- Make the customer the hero
- Address their pain points
- Show empathy and understanding
- Present your labels as the solution

### Conversion Optimization Checklist

- [ ] Hero CTA is above the fold
- [ ] Trust badges are visible on product pages
- [ ] Stock quantities show urgency when low (under 10 items)
- [ ] Product images are high quality and show labels in use
- [ ] Benefits focus on outcomes, not features
- [ ] Mobile menu works smoothly
- [ ] Page loads in under 3 seconds
- [ ] All CTAs use action-oriented language
- [ ] Social proof (reviews) is visible
- [ ] Contact information is easy to find

### Color Psychology

The default colors are chosen for conversion:
- **Blue (Primary)**: Trust, professionalism, reliability
- **Green (Secondary)**: Action, go-ahead, eco-friendly (works well for Australian market)

Consider your brand:
- **Red/Orange**: Urgency, excitement, clearance
- **Purple**: Premium, luxury, creative
- **Navy**: Professional, corporate

## Troubleshooting

### Theme Not Displaying Correctly

1. **Clear cache**: If using a caching plugin, clear all caches
2. **Regenerate CSS**: Go to Customizer and click "Publish" again
3. **Check WooCommerce**: Ensure WooCommerce is active

### Product Pages Not Showing Custom Content

1. Ensure WooCommerce is installed and active
2. Go to WooCommerce → Settings → Products and click "Save changes"
3. This regenerates the product page permalinks

### Mobile Menu Not Working

1. Clear browser cache
2. Check if JavaScript is loading (check browser console)
3. Ensure no JavaScript errors from other plugins

### Images Not Loading

1. Regenerate thumbnails: Install "Regenerate Thumbnails" plugin
2. Run the regeneration process
3. Check file permissions on /wp-content/uploads/

## Performance Optimization

### Recommended Plugins

1. **WP Rocket** or **W3 Total Cache**: Caching
2. **Smush** or **ShortPixel**: Image optimization
3. **Autoptimize**: CSS/JS minification
4. **WP Fastest Cache**: Additional caching layer

### Best Practices

- Keep images under 200KB
- Use WebP format where possible
- Enable browser caching
- Use a CDN for faster delivery
- Limit plugins to essentials

## Best Practices for Australian Small Business Market

### Messaging
- Emphasize local production
- Highlight fast delivery within Australia
- Use Australian spelling (colour, not color - in content)
- Mention support for small batch orders
- Reference Australian business success stories

### Compliance
- Include GST in prices (WooCommerce setting)
- Add privacy policy page (required for Australian businesses)
- Include terms and conditions
- Add Australian Business Number (ABN) in footer

### Payment Options
- Enable popular Australian payment methods
- Consider Afterpay/ZipPay for larger orders
- Accept bank transfer for repeat customers
